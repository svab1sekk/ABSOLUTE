DEEP & SKY
TWO DIRECTIONS. ONE RIVALRY.

TWO WAYS TO WIN.

↓ DEEP DIVE — find the most obscure correct answer. A niche answer takes your submarine deeper.

↑ SKY RISE — give the answer people would think of first. A popular pick lifts your hot-air balloon higher.

  1. Read the question during the 3-second preview. Then you have 25 seconds to submit one answer.
  2. Unknown answers can be retried while time remains. Case, accents, spaces and punctuation are ignored.
  3. Your friend cannot see your answer until both players have submitted or time runs out.
  4. Both players press Ready to continue. There is no round limit. The host can end the run after any round.

Each point moves you 10 metres. A skipped or timed-out answer earns zero.

Scores use fixed, editorial estimates of how familiar answers are. They are not survey results or invented player percentages. Both players always use the same scoring.

Questions use an authored answer bank. For spelling-restricted questions, use the English name. The accepted examples appear after the round.
